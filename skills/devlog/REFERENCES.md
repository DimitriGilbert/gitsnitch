## Structure Rules (Burstiness Enforcement)

### Sentence Length

- Mix these lengths deliberately: 3-word, 8-word, 20-word, 5-word, 35-word
- No two consecutive sentences within 2 words of each other in length
- At least 1 deliberate fragment per paragraph (no verb, no subject, or both)
- After any sentence over 25 words, follow with one under 8 words
- Vary rhythm: never let 3+ sentences follow the same subject-verb pattern

### Paragraph Rules

- No two consecutive paragraphs with the same sentence count
- Vary between 1-sentence punch paragraphs and 5-7 sentence narrative paragraphs
- Opening paragraphs: 1-3 sentences only
- At least one single-word or single-short-sentence paragraph per article
- Technical paragraphs can be longer but must contain a human reaction mid-paragraph

### Section Openings

Rotate between these patterns. Never use the same type twice in a row:
1. **Question** — "Why did the build fail?"
2. **Direct statement** — "The cache was lying."
3. **Anecdote** — "Three hours. That's how long I spent on a typo."
4. **Code + commentary** — Show a snippet, then react to it
5. **Bold claim** — "Most error handling is a waste of code."
6. **Specific data point** — "412 tests. All green. All useless."

## Voice Matching Checklist

After drafting, verify every item:

- [ ] Pronoun usage matches examples (I vs we vs you vs they)

## Opening Line Patterns

### AVOID (never use)

- "This week I..."
- "In this post..."
- "Recently I..."
- "I've been working on..."
- "It's been a while since..."
- "I wanted to share..."
- "Let me tell you about..."
- "Today we're going to..."
- "I'm excited to..."
- "In this article..."
- "Welcome back to..."
- "If you've ever..."

### USE (good patterns)

- **Specific technical observation**: "The memory leak was hiding in the destructor."
- **Emotional reaction**: "I wanted to throw my laptop out the window."
- **Scene-setting**: "2 AM. The tests were green. The staging deploy was red."
- **Direct claim**: "Git bisect is the most underused debugging tool."
- **Question**: "When did 'it works on my machine' stop being an excuse?"
- **Specific data**: "47 open PRs. Zero reviews older than two days."
- **Dialogue/quote**: "'Just ship it,' my PM said. So I did."
- **Action in progress**: "I pressed deploy and immediately opened the logs."
- **Contradiction**: "The fix was a one-liner. Finding it took three days."
- **Confession**: "I rewrote the same function four times this week."

## Closing Line Patterns

### AVOID

- "Until next time..."
- "Stay tuned for..."
- "Let me know what you think..."
- "Thanks for reading..."
- "Happy coding..."
- "That's all for now..."

### USE

- **Unresolved thought**: "I still don't know why it worked on the first try."
- **Forward-looking action**: "Tomorrow I'm deleting the old branch."
- **Specific next step**: "Next up: figuring out why the linter hates destructuring."
- **Circular callback**: "Three hours. Turned out it was a typo after all."
- **Deliberate understatement**: "Could've been worse."
- **Direct opinion**: "TypeScript enums are fine. I will not be taking questions."
